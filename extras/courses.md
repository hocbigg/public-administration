# Great Courses

This curated collection gathers exceptional university lecture series, full OpenCourseWare (OCW) offerings, and authoritative open-learning programs in Public Administration. These courses provide deep conceptual foundations, analytical frameworks, and practical governance insights directly from leading scholars and practitioners.

## Foundations, Ethics, and Public Leadership

- [Justice: What's the Right Thing to Do? (Harvard University / Michael Sandel)](https://pll.harvard.edu/course/justice)
- [The Moral Foundations of Politics (Open Yale Courses / Ian Shapiro)](https://oyc.yale.edu/political-science/plsc-118)
- [Introduction to Political Philosophy (Open Yale Courses / Steven B. Smith)](https://oyc.yale.edu/political-science/plsc-114)
- [Leadership for Purposeful Change: Creating Public Value (Harvard University / edX / Mark Moore)](https://www.edx.org/learn/leadership/harvard-university-leadership-for-purposeful-change)
- [Exercising Leadership: Foundational Principles (Harvard University / edX / Ronald Heifetz)](https://www.edx.org/learn/leadership/harvard-university-exercising-leadership-foundational-principles)
