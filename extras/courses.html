Great Courses

This curated collection gathers exceptional university lecture series, full OpenCourseWare (OCW) offerings, and authoritative open-learning programs in Public Administration. These courses provide deep conceptual foundations, analytical frameworks, and practical governance insights directly from leading scholars and practitioners.

Foundations, Ethics, and Public Leadership

  • Justice: What's the Right Thing to Do? (Harvard University / Michael Sandel)
  • The Moral Foundations of Politics (Open Yale Courses / Ian Shapiro)
  • Introduction to Political Philosophy (Open Yale Courses / Steven B. Smith)
  • Leadership for Purposeful Change: Creating Public Value (Harvard University / edX / Mark Moore)
  • Exercising Leadership: Foundational Principles (Harvard University / edX / Ronald Heifetz)

Public Policy, Economics, and Financial Management

  • Public Finance and Public Policy (MIT OpenCourseWare / Jonathan Gruber)
  • Public Financial Management (International Monetary Fund / edX)
  • Financing and Investing in Infrastructure (Università Bocconi / Coursera / Stefano Gatti)
  • Evaluating Social Programs (MITx / edX / Esther Duflo & Rachel Glennerster)
  • Using Public Policy for Social Change (University of Michigan / Coursera / Barry Checkoway)

Administrative Law, Regulatory Policy, and Environmental Governance

  • Administrative Law [No. 86] (The Federalist Society / YouTube)
  • Environmental Politics and Law (Open Yale Courses / John Wargo)
  • Capitalism: Success, Crisis, and Reform (Open Yale Courses / Douglas W. Rae)
  • Introduction to Key Constitutional Concepts and Supreme Court Cases (University of Pennsylvania / Coursera / Kermit Roosevelt III)

Digital Governance, Innovation, and International Administration

  • Digital Governance (Erasmus University Rotterdam / Coursera / Evert Stamhuis et al.)
  • International Organizations Management (University of Geneva / Coursera / Gilbert Probst & Bruce Jenks)
  • EU Policy and Implementation: Making Europe Work! (Leiden University / Coursera / Bernard Steunenberg)
  • Introduction to Global Governance (KU Leuven / edX / Jan Wouters)
  • Artificial Intelligence in Government (University of Maryland, College Park / Coursera / Joe Mariani)
