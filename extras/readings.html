Great Readings

This curated collection brings together field-defining books, seminal essays, and landmark monographs across the discipline of Public Administration. Rather than standard introductory survey textbooks, these readings represent the conceptual breakthroughs, institutional debates, and enduring controversies that have shaped modern understandings of state power, bureaucratic behavior, public budgeting, and democratic accountability.
