# Advanced Topics

- [Advanced Quantitative Methods and Program Evaluation](#advanced-quantitative-methods-and-program-evaluation)
  (Rigorous causal inference, applied cost-benefit analysis, administrative data science, and mixed-method evaluation frameworks for evidence-based policymaking.)
- [Advanced Public Financial Management and Municipal Finance](#advanced-public-financial-management-and-municipal-finance)
  (Capital budgeting, municipal debt markets, intergovernmental fiscal architecture, and government fiscal condition analysis.)

  (Digital-era state transformation, automated administrative decision-making, algorithmic accountability, and behavioral public administration.)

  (Cross-national bureaucratic traditions, state capacity and autonomy, international organizations, and systemic anti-corruption regimes.)
- [Nonprofit Leadership, Philanthropy, and Hybrid Governance](#nonprofit-leadership-philanthropy-and-hybrid-governance)
  (Strategic philanthropy, board governance, cross-sector service contracting, and hybrid public-private organizational design.)

The tracks below build directly on the Core Undergraduate Curriculum in Public Administration. Learners should select tracks aligned with their research interests or specialized career pathways in public leadership, policy analysis, or third-sector management.

## Advanced Quantitative Methods and Program Evaluation

| Topic | Why Study | Resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Causal Inference & Quasi-Experimental Evaluation | Equips researchers to isolate true program impacts from observational administrative data using difference-in-differences, regression discontinuity, instrumental variables, and synthetic control methods. | [Causal Inference: The Mixtape (Scott Cunningham)](https://mixtape.scunning.com/)<br>- [The Effect: An Introduction to Research Design and Causality (Nick Huntington-Klein)](https://theeffectbook.net/)<br>- Joshua D. Angrist and Jörn-Steffen Pischke, *Mostly Harmless Econometrics: An Empiricist's Companion* (Princeton University Press) |
| Applied Cost-Benefit & Cost-Effectiveness Analysis | Teaches the formal microeconomic frameworks for monetizing impacts, determining social discount rates, valuing non-market goods, and managing risk in large-scale public initiatives. | Anthony E. Boardman, David H. Greenberg, Aidan R. Vining, and David L. Weimer, *Cost-Benefit Analysis: Concepts and Practice* (Cambridge University Press) |
| Computational Public Administration & Administrative Data Science | Explores how modern computational methods, automated text analysis, natural language processing, and big administrative data pipelines can be deployed for regulatory analysis and service monitoring. | [Bit by Bit: Social Research in the Digital Age (Matthew J. Salganik)](https://www.bitbybitbook.com/)<br>- Kenneth Benoit, *Text Analysis in R for Social Science* |
| Mixed Methods & Qualitative Comparative Analysis (QCA) | Integrates qualitative field evidence with small-N set-theoretic comparative tools to diagnose complex causal recipes in organizational reform and policy implementation. | Charles C. Ragin, *Redesigning Social Inquiry: Fuzzy Sets and Beyond* (University of Chicago Press)<br>- John W. Creswell and J. David Creswell, *Research Design: Qualitative, Quantitative, and Mixed Methods Approaches* (SAGE) |

## Advanced Public Financial Management and Municipal Finance

| Topic | Why Study | Resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Capital Budgeting & Infrastructure Finance | Examines multi-year capital improvement plans, life-cycle asset valuation, public infrastructure investment priorities, and project risk allocation. | XiaoHu Wang, *Financial Management in the Public Sector: Tools, Applications, and Cases* (Routledge)<br>- Robert L. Bland, *A Budgeting Guide for Local Government* (ICMA Press) |
| Municipal Debt Markets & Project Finance | Investigates the underwriting, issuance, and rating of general obligation and revenue bonds, tax-exempt debt instruments, and complex project finance structures. | E. | Fiscal Federalism & Subnational Tax Architecture | Analyzes tax incidence, intergovernmental grant design, tax competition among jurisdictions, and optimal assignment of revenue tools across government levels. | Robin Boadway and Anwar Shah, *Fiscal Federalism: Principles and Practices of Multiorder Governance* (Cambridge University Press)<br>- Ronald C. ## Digital Governance, Innovation, and Algorithmic Public Administration

| Topic | Why Study | Resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Digital-Era Governance & Platform Modernization | Analyzes how cloud platforms, service design, shared data infrastructure, and agile methodologies replace traditional bureaucratic silos with citizen-centered public services. | Patrick Dunleavy, Helen Margetts, Simon Bastow, and Jane Tinkler, *Digital Era Governance: IT Corporations, the State, and e-Government* (Oxford University Press)<br>- Mark F. Thompson, *Digital State: How the Internet is Transforming Government* (Oxford University Press) |
| Artificial Intelligence & Algorithmic Accountability | Explores legal, ethical, and administrative implications of automated rulemaking, predictive enforcement models, algorithmic bias, and administrative due process. | David Freeman Engstrom, Daniel E. Ho, Catherine M. | Open Government, Civic Tech & Collective Intelligence | Investigates open data architectures, participatory governance mechanisms, civic crowdsourcing, and the institutional deployment of distributed public knowledge. | Beth Simone Noveck, *Smart Citizens, Smarter State: The Technologies of Expertise and the Future of Governing* (Harvard University Press) |
| Behavioral Public Administration & Nudge Policy | Examines how cognitive biases, heuristics, choice architecture, and behavioral insights inform administrative burden reduction and public service interactions. | Stephan Grimmelikhuijsen, Sebastian Jilke, Asmus Leth Olsen, and Lars Tummers, *Behavioral Public Administration: Combining Insights from Public Administration and Psychology* (Public Administration Review)<br>- Michael Hallsworth and Elspeth Kirkman, *Behavioral Insights* (MIT Press) |
